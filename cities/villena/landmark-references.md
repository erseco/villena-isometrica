# Landmark References — Villena

Curated photographic references from Wikimedia Commons for visual quality assurance and architectural verification of prominent landmarks in the Area of Interest (AOI). Links only; no files are downloaded or committed.

---

## 1. Castillo de la Atalaya

A 12th-century fortress of Islamic origin renovated in the 15th century, featuring a massive rectangular keep (*Torre del Homenaje*), a double crenellated curtain wall, and circular corner towers.

- [Castillo de la Atalaya 0293C.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Castillo_de_la_Atalaya_0293C.jpg)  
  *Author:* B25es · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Shows the prominent 4-storey rectangular keep with its flat, crenellated roof terrace and circular corner sentry turrets rising above the double curtain walls.
- [Sierra de la Villa. Villena. Castillo de la Atalaya 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sierra_de_la_Villa._Villena._Castillo_de_la_Atalaya_1.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Elevated view from the Sierra de la Villa demonstrating the outer polygonal wall with cylindrical towers and the rocky terrain upon which the fortress is anchored.
- [1935. Villena. Castillo de la Atalaya.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1935._Villena._Castillo_de_la_Atalaya.jpg)  
  *Author:* Archivo Histórico Provincial de Alicante (Fotografía histórica) · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Historic perspective displaying the masonry texture, elevation above the historic urban fabric, and barbican layout before late 20th-century restorations.

## 2. Iglesia Arciprestal de Santiago

A late Gothic and Renaissance 15th–16th century church, the most significant historic temple in Villena, featuring three naves, spiralled helical columns, and a square bell tower.

- [Villena. Iglesia de Santiago 2.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Iglesia_de_Santiago_2.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Highlights the massive quadrangular bell tower, high gabled nave roof structure, and stone masonry exterior.
- [1878. Iglesia de Santiago. Alzado.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1878._Iglesia_de_Santiago._Alzado.jpg)  
  *Author:* Archivo de Villena · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Architectural elevation plan showing the roof ridge levels, side buttresses, and the proportional height of the bell tower relative to the main vault.
- [Villena. Iglesia de Santiago. Escudo de Villena.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Iglesia_de_Santiago._Escudo_de_Villena.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Detailed view of the north façade portal, cornices, and decorative stonework facing Plaza de Santiago.

## 3. Iglesia de Santa María

A 16th-century Gothic-Renaissance church built upon a former mosque, featuring a single nave, lateral chapels between buttresses, and a baroque bell tower.

- [Vista de la Iglesia de Santa María (Villena).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vista_de_la_Iglesia_de_Santa_Mar%C3%ADa_(Villena).jpg)  
  *Author:* José Luis Filpo Cabana · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Exterior view illustrating the bell tower topped with a four-sided pyramidal tile roof, the tiled nave roof, and its position over Plaza de Santa María.
- [1936~. Villena. Iglesia de Santa María. Exterior.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1936~._Villena._Iglesia_de_Santa_Mar%C3%ADa._Exterior.jpg)  
  *Author:* Archivo Histórico · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Historic perspective showing the side chapels, buttress geometry, and rectangular layout before urban alterations.

## 4. Ayuntamiento de Villena (Palacio Municipal)

A 16th-century Renaissance palace attributed to Jacobo Florentino, former house of the municipal council and current town hall.

- [Ayuntamiento villena.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ayuntamiento_villena.jpg)  
  *Author:* Danielby · *Licence:* CC BY 2.5  
  *Visual features:* Frontal view of the three-storey Renaissance façade with ashlar masonry, classical portico entrance, iron balconies, and low hipped tiled roof.
- [Frontón del Ayuntamiento de Villena.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Front%C3%B3n_del_Ayuntamiento_de_Villena.JPG)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 3.0  
  *Visual features:* Detailed upper cornice and pediment with the city coat of arms, demonstrating roof eaves line.
- [1920. Villena. Palacio Municipal.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1920._Villena._Palacio_Municipal.JPG)  
  *Author:* Archivo Histórico · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Early 20th-century view demonstrating the adjacent alleyway connections and relative height compared to neighbouring houses in the old quarter.

## 5. Gran Teatro Chapí

An eclectic historicist theater inaugurated in 1925, dedicated to the composer Ruperto Chapí, featuring a neoclassical/modernist façade with arched galleries.

- [Teatro Chapi Villena 01.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teatro_Chapi_Villena_01.JPG)  
  *Author:* Superchilum · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Main entrance façade showing the monumental double arched portico, central sculpture niches, and upper pediment crowning the entrance.
- [Teatro Chapi Villena 03.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teatro_Chapi_Villena_03.JPG)  
  *Author:* Superchilum · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 4.0  
  *Visual features:* Corner perspective depicting the elevated fly tower (*caja escénica*) rising behind the auditorium with its higher flat roof volume.
- [1930~. Villena. Teatro Chapí 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1930~._Villena._Teatro_Chap%C3%AD_1.jpg)  
  *Author:* Archivo Histórico · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Historic view from Paseo Chapí showing the perimeter gardens and tree canopy framing the building façade.

## 6. Plaza Mayor

Historic urban square of medieval origin with porticoed sections, traditional whitewashed façades, and a 19th-century monumental central fountain.

- [Villena. Plaza Mayor. Fuente decimonónica.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Plaza_Mayor._Fuente_decimon%C3%B3nica.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 3.0  
  *Visual features:* Depicts the enclosed polygonal square perimeter, 2-to-3 storey vernacular houses with pitched ceramic roofs, and central cast-iron fountain.
- [Villena. Plaza Mayor. Fuente y arco.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Plaza_Mayor._Fuente_y_arco.JPG)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 3.0  
  *Visual features:* Shows the covered stone archway (*Arco de la Plaza Mayor*) providing street access beneath residential buildings.
- [1935. Villena. Plaza del Mercado (plaza Mayor).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1935._Villena._Plaza_del_Mercado_(plaza_Mayor).jpg)  
  *Author:* Archivo Histórico · *Licence:* Public Domain  
  *Visual features:* Historic view showing market stalls and the elevation gradient of the square climbing towards the castle hill.

## 7. Estación de Ferrocarril de Villena

The central railway passenger station on the Madrid–Alicante line (opened 1858 by MZA), located on the western boundary of the AOI.

- [Villena. Estación de la RENFE. Dirección sur.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Estaci%C3%B3n_de_la_RENFE._Direcci%C3%B3n_sur.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 3.0  
  *Visual features:* Trackside view of the elongated 2-storey passenger passenger terminal with pitched ceramic roof, canopied passenger platform, and parallel rail lines.
- [Villena. Desde Villena AV 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villena._Desde_Villena_AV_1.jpg)  
  *Author:* Qoan (Enrique Íñiguez Rodríguez) · *Licence:* CC BY-SA 3.0  
  *Visual features:* General urban context from the rail corridor looking east towards the town center and castle ridge.
